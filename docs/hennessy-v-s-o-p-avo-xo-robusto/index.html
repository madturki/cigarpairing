Hennessy VSOP & Avo XO Robusto

It's late into the night with my pair pals and I made the choice after a great CAO gold label to try my AVO xo with Hennessy V.S.O.P.

This was a knock out pair.

The first draw on the AVO was fantastic, full of flavor with no bite what so ever. One of the best medium bodied Dominican cigars I've had. Smooth and creamy with elegant woody flavors and hints of coffee.

If you're a complex full body cigar smoker, this won't blow you away, but you won't be able to deny its great taste.

After the first third I poured the Hennessy and took a sip. The Hennessy was sweet, warm and also full of flavor. As I went back and forth from the cigar to the drink, the pair kept getting better. I didn't ash till part way through the final third, finished up the drink just before putting out the cigar.

Such a smooth combo, randomly discovered.

Everyone left for the night but I still enjoyed the company of two new friends...and I'm sure we'll hang out again.

If you're looking for a pair that's smooth and sweet, try this combo...

It is a new favorite!

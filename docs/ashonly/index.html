#ashOnly Contest Rules

Show us your longest ash!  Post an original photo between July 18, 2013 to July 27 2013 with the longest ash you can produce from a single cigar!

The ash does not need to be attached but each break in the ash will be considered towards your success.  Choose a long cigar!

Post as often as you want but remember you're going for the longest ash so the longest one possible is what you're going for!  Tag #ashOnly and @cigarsspirits on your entries so we can find them.  Contest runs from July 18, 2013 to July 27 2013.  Winner will be chose by Carlito's Stogie Nubber and Cigars & Spirits.  Winner will be announced on July 28 2013.  Original photos only.  Photos must have been posted after July 18 2013.  Not following these rules could mean disqualification from the contest!

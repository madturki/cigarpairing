Grammworks Giveaway

September 20, 2013

Want to win a premium quality Grammworks travel humidor?  You bet you do!

Submit your video by October 12th 2013 by uploading to Facebook, Twitter or Instagram.  Hashtag it with #GWvideo and make sure you're following @cigarsspirits and @grammworks!  The best videos will be re-shared, posted on our site and may be used in promotions by Grammworks in the future!

Your video should be well planned, shot well and include one of the following themes:

1) Embargo
2) Humidors
3) BOTL

Sitting in your back yard lighting one up probably wont be enough!  Get your friends involved and make it awesome!  The winner will receive a 6" premium quality travel humidor from grammworks.com!

Ready?  Go!

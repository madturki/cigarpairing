Cigars & Spirits does Movember

November 1, 2013

It's that time of the year again where men all over the world will either look distinguished, or dirty.  But it's all for a great cause - to raise awareness for the issues surrounding men's health, and to raise funds in support of cancer research.

How Movember Works

At the beginning of November participants will shave off all facial hair regardless of how much that had previously or how long they've had it.  Then you grow.  You grow any moustache you'd like.  Remember though, once a moustache connects to the beard, it's simply a beard - not a moustache.

As people ask why you look either so handsome or so ridiculous you can reply "I'm doing for men's health!" And then proceed to name off some statistics on men's health.

Next, ask them for a donation!  You can direct them either to your individual page, or your team page at http://moteam.co/cigarsspirits

Why Join Our Team?

We're passionate about this cause and think you should be too.  By joining our team you'll be part of a larger success story and if you're a top contributor in our team you'll be rewarded with a sweet prize pack provided by all of our friends.

So what are you waiting for!?  Join the Cigars & Spirits Movember team!

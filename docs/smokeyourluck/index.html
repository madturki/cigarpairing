Smoke Your Luck

Time for another awesome giveaway from Cigars & Spirits and Crimson Lord Cigars!

Post your themed cigar photo between now and the end of St Patrick's day, March 17th 2014, and you could win a 10-pack of Lucky Ten cigars by Crimson Lord Cigars.

Your photo must include one of the following three things: a clover, a beer, or something green.  Finally, tag your photo #smokeyourluck and #cigarporn and make sure you're following both @cigarsspirits and @crimsonlordcigars on Instagram!

Winner will be chosen by Cigars & Spirits and Crimson Lord Cigars shortly after the 17th.  Contest open to US citizens only.  Contest closes March 17th 2014 at midnight EST.

Stores

This is a list of places we like to shop at and why we think you should too!

Village Cigar Company
Burlington, Ontario

We love these guys because of the great atmosphere.  In an industry steeped in tradition these guys show respect to the product and their clients with how they run their shop.  With a great selection, great company and a great area to smoke in in the back, this is one of our favorite places to go.  Watch for frequent events occurring in their shop.  we're happy to shop with them any day.

Celebrity Tobacco and Gifts
Oakville, Ontario

This place has a huge selection and a large walk-in humidor.  Everything from the cheapies to the finest and rarest cigars around, the owner is more than an entrepreneur but a collector and enthusiast.  You'll always find great advice and suggestions.  They also have a great array of humidors, cutters, books, lighters, pipes, tobacco and everything the aficionado needs to make an impression.

Virgil Ave  Tobacconist
Buffalo, New York

This is truly is a tobacconist.  Their main product is tobacco and just the essential tools to enjoy it.  Antonio is knowledgeable and friendly with a great sense of humor.  This shop features one of the less than 40 official Rocky Patel Lounges in the world.  Because of this they are also one of the few places you can get the coveted Rocky Patel "Burn".  Their walk-in humidor is full stocked with great smokes from around the world - well, not Cubans...  The lounge is a great place to try out your new product while watching a game and having a beer.

Tobacco Barn
Lake Forest, California

The Tobacco Barn has been in business for nearly 100 years. Their philosophy is "create a comfortable place to enjoy a wide selection of quality cigars or pipes with friends" and they've done just that.  In addition to all the great cigars, pipes and accessories they offer, their location boasts a phenomenal members' area that includes a 14'  widescreen projection system (along with several other TVs), shuffleboard, dart boards and a simulator room to work on your golf swing.  We met up with @masfy while there and will be sure to visit again!
